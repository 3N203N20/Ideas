Field guide

How to play Skulk Chess

Board & pieces. Skulk Chess uses an 11×11 board, 22 pieces per side and the back-rank order R N B B Q K N B B N R. Pieces otherwise move as in chess.

Captures spread Skulk. After a capture, the capturer places one permanent Skulk block on an empty square adjacent (including diagonally) to any existing Skulk. If no placement is possible, the capture still resolves.

Exposure. At the end of each piece owner's turn, each of their pieces still on Skulk gains one exposure. Opponent turns do not count. When exposure reaches 5, the piece is destroyed immediately at the end of its owner's turn, before any required Skulk placement. Moving to another Skulk square preserves exposure; ending a move on a normal square resets it.

Kings are vulnerable. Kings can enter Skulk and can be destroyed by reaching exposure 5. A king destroyed by Skulk loses the game. Kings also follow normal check and checkmate rules.

Pawns. Pawns move one square forward (two on their first move), capture diagonally and may capture en passant. White may promote on ranks 9 or 10 or must promote on 11. Black may promote on ranks 3 or 2 or must promote on 1. Promotion choices are Queen, Rook, Bishop or Knight; optional ranks also allow keeping the pawn.

Castling. The king begins on F. Kingside: F→H and K-rook→G. Queenside: F→D and A-rook→E. Standard castling safety and unmoved-piece conditions apply. Skulk on the back rank does not block castling.

Winning & draws. Win by checkmate or by destroying the opposing king with Skulk. Stalemate, threefold repetition, the 50-move rule, resignation and an agreed draw end the game.

Notation. $ marks a new Skulk block and ~ marks Skulk destruction. An asterisk (*) is attached for every exposure counter: for example, Kk2*** means the king on k2 has accumulated three exposure turns. This is recorded for every piece that gains exposure. A piece reaching five is destroyed immediately and is marked with ~ instead of showing 5/5. Check (+) and checkmate (#) markers are attached to every move or Skulk-destruction entry that contributes to that effect.
